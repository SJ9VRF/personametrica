# Three-minute demo script

**0:00–0:25 — Problem.** Most assistants can retrieve history; the harder problem is maintaining a changing model of a person and deciding when that state should affect behavior.

**0:25–0:55 — Learn.** Enter: “I prefer concise answers. My goal is finish my conference talk by Friday. I prefer working in the evening.” Show Memory and Goals.

**0:55–1:25 — Act with calibration.** Advance the scenario toward the deadline. Show a low-risk suggestion and open the Why view: goal relevance, urgency, confidence, stakes, reversibility, interruptibility, and historical acceptance.

**1:25–1:50 — Change.** Enter: “Actually, mornings work better for me now.” Show the old evening preference as superseded and morning as active rather than keeping contradictory active memories.

**1:50–2:15 — Verify.** Execute a sandbox tool action, inject a mismatched outcome, and show the verifier detect it instead of assuming the action succeeded.

**2:15–2:40 — Measure.** Open the dashboard. Compare the full system with targeted ablations: no temporal update, no contradiction resolution, uncalibrated proactivity, and no verification.

**2:40–3:00 — Close.** “The goal is not a chatbot with a vector database. It is a closed-loop research substrate for learning, remembering, acting, measuring failures, and turning them into the next training signal.”
