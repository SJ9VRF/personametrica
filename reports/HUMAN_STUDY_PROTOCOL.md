# Human Longitudinal Study Protocol — Not Yet Executed

## Purpose

Evaluate whether PersonaMetrica improves longitudinal usefulness and personalization while controlling memory errors, intrusive intervention, and user corrections compared with a non-temporal baseline.

## Design

Proposed randomized crossover study with 20 adult participants over 7 days. Each participant uses two conditions on counterbalanced task blocks: (A) temporal PersonaMetrica and (B) retrieval/first-mention baseline. The study should be preregistered before data collection.

## Tasks

Participants bring low-stakes planning and productivity tasks such as preparing a presentation, planning recurring work, maintaining preferences for answer format, and tracking a small multi-day goal. No high-risk medical, financial, employment, or legal decisions should be included.

## Primary outcomes

- user-rated usefulness per session;
- personalization accuracy on explicit preference checks;
- stale-memory error rate after preference reversal;
- accepted proactive intervention precision;
- correction rate per 10 interactions.

## Secondary outcomes

- perceived intrusiveness;
- trust/calibration rating;
- goal progress;
- memory deletion/correction usage;
- latency and interaction cost.

## Procedure

Day 0 establishes baseline preferences and goal state. Days 1–5 include repeated task interactions and at least one scripted preference change. Day 6 includes held-out scenarios. Day 7 includes a retrospective interview and memory-inspection task.

## Analysis

Use participant-level paired comparisons for crossover outcomes, report confidence intervals/effect sizes, and separately analyze pre/post preference reversal windows. Do not pool repeated interactions as independent samples without participant-level modeling.

## Safety and privacy

Collect only study-required data. Provide memory inspection/deletion controls. Avoid sensitive tasks. Separate raw interaction data from participant identifiers. Obtain informed consent and institutional review as required before execution.

## Status

**Unexecuted.** This file is a protocol artifact only; the repository contains no fabricated human-study results.
